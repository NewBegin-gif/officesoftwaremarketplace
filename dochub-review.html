Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 18 Nov 2026

DocHub has four plans. Free costs €0. Its core features, which are editing existing text, self sign, sending sign requests, templates, envelopes and sharing by link, all draw on one limit of 3 uses a month, next to basic PDF tools, the AI Assistant and the mobile apps; the comparison table also caps Free at 3 folders. Basic is €8 a month billed yearly or €11 billed monthly.

It makes self sign, PDF downloads, templates and folders unlimited and adds advanced fillable fields and document attachments, but editing existing text, sign requests and envelopes stay inside the limit of 3 uses a month. Pro is €12 a month billed yearly or €16 billed monthly and makes PDF editing, sign requests and envelopes unlimited, with bulk send, in-person signing and the DocHub Intelligence tools on top.

Site License is priced through sales and adds unlimited users, single sign-on, and HIPAA with a BAA available. The yearly switch says it saves 25%, and in an organization each member who needs Pro features is paid for. Pro has a 30-day free trial. At checkout on 18 November 2026 it asked for a card through Stripe and said the first payment of €16.00 would be due on 18 December 2026, plus taxes, unless the trial is cancelled before that date; the yearly Pro checkout showed €12 a month billed as €144 a year. We could not read US dollar prices. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What DocHub does

Online PDF editor and e-signature tool: edit text and fields in a PDF, sign it yourself and send sign requests, with a free plan capped at a few uses a month.

Basic frees your own signing, Pro frees sending

On the free plan every core action draws on the same 3 uses a month, so it works as a trial more than a working plan. Basic is the step for people who mostly sign their own paperwork: self sign, PDF downloads, templates and folders have no cap there. Sending documents to other people is still capped at Basic, because sign requests and envelopes stay inside the 3 uses. Pro is where sending opens up, with unlimited sign requests and envelopes, bulk send and in-person signing.

Paying yearly takes Basic from €11 to €8 a month and Pro from €16 to €12, so a year of Pro came to €144 at our checkout. An organization pays for each member who needs Pro, so price it per person before you choose.

    How to actually use DocHub

    Sign up for the free plan; it asked for no card in our test. On the dashboard, Import a Document opens your PDF in the editor, where you can add text, fillable fields and a date, then sign it yourself under Sign, Create Signatures, by phone, drawing, an uploaded image or a typed name. Our first signed document took under two minutes. Each of those actions can count toward the 3 uses a month on the free plan.

    For unlimited sign requests you need Pro. Its 30-day trial asks for a card at checkout. To stop it, go to Settings, Billing, Change Plan, choose Cancel Subscription and turn off automatic renewal before the date the checkout names; the pricing FAQ says the account then moves to the free plan.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from DocHub?

    How much does DocHub cost?

    Read on 18 November 2026 in euros, before tax: Free is €0, Basic €8 a month on a yearly bill or €11 month to month, Pro €12 yearly or €16 month to month, and Site License is quoted by sales. We could not see US dollar prices.

    What does the free plan really allow?

    A handful of actions a month. Editing existing text, self sign, sign requests, templates, envelopes and link sharing all share one limit of 3 uses. In our test we uploaded, edited and signed a PDF on the free plan, and the screen did not tell us how many uses that took.

    Can I send documents for others to sign without Pro?

    A few. Free and Basic both list sending sign requests, inside the same limit of 3 uses a month. Pro makes sign requests unlimited. The Send for Signature row in the table, Pro only, is an AI Assistant feature that helps prepare requests.

    Does the Pro trial need a card?

    Yes, in our test. The checkout asked for card details through Stripe before the 30-day trial could start and named 18 December 2026 as the date of the first payment of €16.00. It said nothing is charged if you cancel before then. The free plan needed no card.

    How do I cancel the trial?

    The pricing FAQ says: Settings, Billing, Change Plan, Cancel Subscription, then turn off automatic renewal, after which the account moves to the free plan. We stopped before starting a trial, so we only have the FAQ's word for this route.

    Are DocHub signatures legally valid?

    DocHub claims legal validity on its own signature screen. We did not check that claim against the law of any country, so ask your adviser if a contract depends on it.
