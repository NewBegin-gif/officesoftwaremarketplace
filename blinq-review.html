The catch

And a truth about all digital cards: the wow factor works precisely because adoption is still low; the durable value is the CRM sync (contacts flowing automatically into your pipeline), so judge Business tier by that integration, not by the tap-to-share trick.

What Blinq does

Digital business cards shared by QR code or link, with contacts syncing into your CRM. A free plan covers two cards; Business is about $4.99/user/month annual with a five-user minimum, and the event lead-capture add-on starts near $199/month.

What they praise

When the paid plan is worth it

Blinq's free tier gives you two digital business cards at $0, which covers a solo user who only needs to hand over contact details. Premium at $9.99 per month, or $7.33 per month billed annually, raises that to five cards and adds branded QR codes, custom design and the AI Notetaker. Worth it if your card is a client-facing asset that has to match your brand, and not otherwise. The genuinely large jump is Premium to Business, at $6.99 per card per month billed monthly or $4.99 billed annually, with a minimum of five team cards.

That is where the admin platform, user management and admin-controlled email signatures sit, so Business is priced for the person who has to keep a team's cards consistent, not for the person carrying one. Enterprise is custom-priced and is the only tier with enforced SSO and SCIM provisioning. Upgrading does not fix control of the rollout: the help centre states that only the workspace owner can set up and manage integrations, and central signature sync is documented for Google Workspace only. Premium offers a 7-day free trial, Business a 30-day one.

How to actually use Blinq

The Business admin onboarding guide lays out a four-step checklist, in order. Create a Team Template with your logo, colours and field permissions. Add team members by email, shareable link, bulk CSV, SSO or SCIM. Create Team Cards from the template and send activation emails, then connect your CRM under Settings > Integrations. Setup is short; the friction comes afterwards. Activation links expire — the help centre states the link in the claim email is only valid for 7 days, so any batch you send and forget has to be resent card by card.

Enforced SSO is not self-serve: Blinq asks you to email Support a list of domains with an example address for each, then approves them, and you may be required to validate ownership via DNS verification. Central email signature sync needs Google Workspace Super Admin access, and Google advises allowing up to 24 hours to install; no equivalent Microsoft 365 deployment is documented. Template images must be cropped to 16:9, and templates cannot be built from the mobile apps.

The natural comparison is HiHello or Mobilo — the digital-card peer set — compare CRM integrations and whether you need hardware (NFC cards) versus pure app.

Frequently asked questions

What does Blinq cost?

Free for individuals with two cards. Premium for individuals is $9.99 a month, or $7.33 billed annually. Business for teams is $6.99 per user a month, or $4.99 per user annually, with a five-user minimum. Read on 17 August 2026.

Why is the team plan cheaper per person than the individual one?

Because it is priced for volume: $4.99 per user annually against $7.33 for a single Premium seat. The catch is the five-user minimum, so a team of three pays for five. Between three and five people the arithmetic is close — above five, the team tier wins on both price and features.

What does the events add-on cost?

Lead capture for events starts around $199 a month, which is a different order of magnitude from the card plans and is priced for trade-show use rather than everyday networking. Budget it per event season rather than as a permanent line.

What should I weigh before buying IT & Productivity software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
