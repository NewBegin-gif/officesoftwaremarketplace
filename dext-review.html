Dext Review (2026)From $25/mo for 250 Documents

By Daan Haket · Tested hands-on · written with an AI assistant · who does what

Dext is a data-capture layer, not accounting software — it feeds Xero, QuickBooks or Sage, so you still need one of those.

Best for

accountants, bookkeepers and growing businesses drowning in receipts who want fast, accurate capture flowing straight into Xero, QuickBooks or Sage.

Skip it if

you're a sole trader with few receipts (pricey for low volume), or you want all-in-one accounting — Dext only does the capture layer.

We tested it · 2026-10-21

Tested by our team on 21 October 2026 during the 14-day trial, without a card and without connecting accounting software or a bank: Daan created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in Daan's browser. We uploaded two made-up receipts whose every field we knew, then a byte-identical copy of one and a rotated copy of the other.

How we tested

  1. Recorded the extraction allowances after trial onboarding
  2. Inspected the Costs upload modes and document settings
  3. Submitted two synthetic receipts to the Costs inbox
  4. Compared the recognised receipt fields with our reference values
  5. Resubmitted a byte copy and a rotated receipt
  • Both receipts reached the Costs inbox within about a minute, and all eight fields we checked were right: date, supplier, total and tax on each (EUR 12.10 with EUR 2.10 tax, and EUR 21.80 with EUR 1.80 tax). Dext picked the category itself: 'Office Supplies' for the stationery receipt and 'Travel' for the transport one.
  • The rotated copy was marked 'Duplicate' in Submission history and kept out of the inbox, which is right, but it still counted: the usage counter went from 0 to 3 of 250 for two receipts.
  • The byte-identical copy appeared nowhere, not even in Submission history: it was dropped without a message.

Dext has moved off named tiers: you choose a region and a billing period, then slide an allowance. The base of 250 documents a month and 5 users costs $25.21 per month billed annually ($302.50 a year) or $31.50 monthly in the Rest of the World, £30 monthly in the UK, €31.50 in the EU and A$42 in APAC, all excluding tax.

The annual discount is stated as up to 20% everywhere except the UK page, which says up to 19%. Add-ons are billed separately: AI Assist $10.5 a month until 31 January 2027 (the page said 31 October when we read it on 21 August 2026), Commerce Lite from $7.50 and Vault free for 100MB then from $4. Line Item Extraction and Supplier Statement Extraction are from $20.50 a month or $0.50 per document, and Bank Statement Extraction from $13.00 a month or $0.32 per document.

Plans include 10 sheets of bank statement extraction, 5 line-item documents and 5 supplier statement extractions as free credits, and the free trial is 14 days with no payment details required. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

Dext no longer sells plan tiers, so there is nothing to upgrade to: you pick a region and a billing period, then drag a slider for documents and users. On Rest of the World the base is 250 documents a month and 5 users at $25.21 a month billed annually, $302.50 up front, or $31.50 if you pay monthly. The headline figure is the annual one, and the gap is exactly the 20% the page advertises.

Region moves the number more than any choice you make. The same allowance is 30 pounds in the UK, 31.50 euro in the EU and 42 dollars AUD in APAC, and the UK page offers 19% for annual where Rest of the World offers 20%. A figure quoted for one market tells you little about another. What spending more does not buy is capability. Line item extraction, supplier statement extraction, bank statement extraction and AI Assist are separate add-ons from $13.00 to $20.50 a month; the plan carries only 5, 5 and 10 free credits of them. Sliding the document allowance up never unlocks those.

How to actually use Dext

Signup runs through app.dext.com/en/signups: pick For businesses and start the 14-day free trial, which the help centre says needs no payment details. The first sign-in opens a setup checklist, and two items on it decide whether the rest works. Connect your accounting software under Business settings then Connections, for Xero, QuickBooks Online or Sage, because that import brings in your chart of accounts and contacts and pushes extracted data back on publish.

Without it Dext still captures documents, but the help centre routes you to a manual export instead. Then download the mobile app, which is where receipts get captured day to day. Documents reach Dext three ways: the app, drag-and-drop upload in the web app, or a unique forwarding address per user that you can hand to suppliers. Set supplier rules early. They fix the account code, tax rate and payment method per supplier, and auto-publish only earns its keep once that coding is right.

Frequently asked questions

Does Dext's trial require a credit card?

Sign-up check 2026-10-21: card not requested in this account; trial length confirmed: 14 days.

What happens when I hit the monthly document limit?

You can keep uploading, but Dext stops extracting data from those documents until your next bill date or until you upgrade, according to the FAQ on dext.com/en/business/pricing (read 10 October 2026). The help centre shows documents processed and remaining under Business settings > Subscription > View usage, so check that before a busy month rather than after. The base allowance is 250 documents a month; more is bought by moving the slider, at a price the page shows per region. In our trial on 21 October 2026 the counter read '0 / 250, Resets in 1 days', and a receipt Dext flagged as a duplicate still counted towards it.

Who counts as a user, and what does an extra one cost?

The base plan always includes 5 users, and each Business Single User Add-On adds one more user together with extra documents and credits (help centre, "Common questions about your Dext business account", read 10 October 2026). Neither that article nor the pricing page prints the add-on's price, so that is not confirmed. You change users and switch between monthly and annual billing under Business settings > Subscription > Change plan.

How do I cancel, and what happens to my receipts afterwards?

Only a user with the Business Admin role can cancel. An annual plan must be cancelled at least 30 days before the renewal date; after that window it renews (help centre, "Cancelling your Dext account", read 10 October 2026). Access runs to the end of the paid period; after that extraction stops but you can still sign in and download your data. Dext says it reserves the right to delete accounts after a subscription has expired and recommends exporting first, so download images and the CSV of extracted data in the web app before you cancel. If you bought through the App Store or Google Play, cancel there; if your accountant pays, they cancel.
