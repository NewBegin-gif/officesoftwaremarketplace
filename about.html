About this site: the numbers behind the numbers

42 finance, HR and office software reviews written by someone who spent twenty years watching where the fees hide.

Why a finance and HR software site, of all things

For twenty years my job was the plumbing of money: trade finance operations and later risk & control at ABN AMRO and Deutsche Bank, running service teams in markets like Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Pakistan and Russia. You learn one thing in that world that software marketing never tells you — the cost of a tool is not the number on the pricing page. It is the number after the seat minimums, the per-employee tiers, the payroll country fees and the annual-only billing kick in.

This site exists because that is exactly where office, finance and HR software hides its real price. Every review here starts from the vendor’s own pricing page, read on a stated date, and works forward to what a small team actually pays.

What is on this site, counted

  • 42full reviews, canonical here
  • 7buying guides and blueprints

Plus a cost-trap atlas for this niche, a straight-answers page per tool, and a tool finder. Nothing here is a copy of another site in the network: when a review moves here, this becomes its only full version.

The filter every finance review passes through

  • What does it cost at 10 people, not at 1? Payroll, EOR and expense tools price per head; the demo price is almost never the real one.
  • Which fee is missing from the pricing page? FX spreads, per-country payroll surcharges, card issuance — if I found one, it is in the review with the page I found it on.
  • Who should not buy this? A review without an honest ‘skip it if’ is an advertisement.
  • Would I renew? The only question that survives a year of use.

The same rules as the rest of the network

Numbers go stale. If a price, a seat cap or a fee on this site no longer matches the vendor’s page, write to daniel@aibuildermarketplace.com and I will check it against the source and fix it within two days — the source link stays on the page so you can check me.

Nearly every link to a vendor is an affiliate link. When you buy through one, the vendor pays me; you pay the same. I will not tilt a verdict for that money, because a review you cannot trust earns nothing at all.

I write with AI assistance — Claude, plus an agent of my own — for drafting, structure and translation. What goes live has been used, checked against my own figures and edited by me; unedited model text does not get published.
