Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 7 Nov 2026

AML Watcher sells anti-money-laundering screening in three plans: Basic for quick checks, Premium for in-depth risk analysis and enhanced due diligence, and Enterprise for large organizations. The monthly or yearly amount is calculated by a slider for searches per month and entities monitored, and the page text we read does not show those amounts, so we do not quote a plan price.

What the page does show is a one-time set-up cost of $250 for Basic and $1,000 for Premium, with Enterprise on a custom quote, and a saving of 17% for yearly billing. The minimum plan includes 100 monitored entities. A free trial gives the first five searches free. Extra searches above a package are charged at the end of the billing cycle at your agreed per-unit rate, unused credits do not roll over, and screening plus monitoring the same customer counts as one monitored entity in a month.

Cancellation is done by emailing the sales team, and access continues to the end of the billing cycle. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What AML Watcher does

Anti-money-laundering screening for sanctions, PEP and adverse media through a dashboard or API, with ongoing monitoring and a free trial.

Premium for bulk screening and monitoring

Basic covers a single team member with one search profile and a limit of one API call per second. Premium adds bulk screening of up to 50 profiles at a time, ongoing monitoring, more team members and faster API limits, which matters once you screen customers in batches or must watch them continuously. The $750 difference in set-up cost is the part to weigh before you decide.

    How to actually use AML Watcher

    Open amlwatcher.com and start the free trial, which includes the first five searches. Run a sanctions, PEP or adverse-media search on a name to see the results, then choose Basic or Premium and set the monthly search volume and number of monitored entities on the pricing page. API users can connect the screening to their own onboarding flow. An enterprise plan or a customised setup goes through a call with the sales team. We did not run searches ourselves.
