What the price did

We capture ChemiCloud’s own pricing page every month and keep the figures, so this is our record of the page — not a forecast, and not someone else’s summary.

This is the clearest price move we have on record. Our July and August 2026 captures of ChemiCloud's hosting page are byte-for-byte identical: Starter $2.49, Pro $3.49 and Turbo $4.49 a month, renewing at $11.95, $17.95 and $21.95. When we re-read the page on 17 August 2026 every intro price had dropped by a dollar: $1.49, $2.49 and $3.49, advertised as up to 88% off for the company's tenth birthday. The annual offer had gone from two months free to six. On 2 September 2026 the birthday page was still up with hours left on its clock, while the September campaign that replaces it was already published at $1.95, $2.95 and $3.49 a month, above the birthday rate and below the July and August one. The three renewal prices were unchanged in all four readings. That is the whole shape of the deal: the discount is on the term you are buying, not on the price you will keep paying.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Support and performance reviews are genuinely strong for the price band, but this is shared/managed hosting — if you need serious dedicated resources or enterprise SLAs, you'll outgrow the cheap tiers. Great value to start, just enter with eyes open on renewal.

What ChemiCloud does

Lightning-fast cloud hosting with free migrations, daily backups, and 24/7 expert support. Perfect balance of performance and affordability for growing businesses.

What buyers report about ChemiCloud

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run ChemiCloud ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Independent testers measure uptime around 99.8-99.9% and load times near 306ms — the performance is real.
  • • Your primary domain renews free for as long as hosting is active. Most hosts charge $15-$20 a year for that.
  • • HostAdvice's own tested review, updated 10 August 2026, measured a test site loading in under two seconds, an instant live-chat reply and a 16-minute ticket response — support speed is the thing every source agrees on.
What they complain about
  • • The renewal is the thing. Starter's promotional rate — $2.49/month, cut to $1.49 for the birthday sale running to 31 August 2026 — renews at $11.95. Budget the renewal figure, not the intro one.
  • • Features that other hosts include come as paid add-ons here.
  • • Reviewers report gradual performance degradation since 2021, worst in Q4 2025.
  • • The 40 GB storage ceiling rules out anything media-heavy.
  • • Two constraints from the same tested review that the price list does not surface: there is no monthly billing option, so the advertised per-month figures come attached to a longer term, and latency is noticeably higher outside Europe and North America, where you would need a CDN on top.

When the paid plan is worth it

The shared tiers split on website count first, resources second. Starter, $1.95/mo on the September Back to Build sale, holds one website, 20 GB NVMe storage, 1 CPU core, 2 GB memory and 250,000 inodes. The moment a second site is needed, Pro at $2.95/mo is the only route: it raises the limit to unlimited websites and adds 35 GB, 2 cores and 4 GB. Turbo at $3.49/mo adds 50 GB, 3 cores, 6 GB, 30-day backup retention and advanced malware protection, and is aimed at sites ChemiCloud rates at roughly 100,000 monthly visits rather than the 25,000 it assigns Starter.

Beyond that band the ladder leaves shared hosting entirely: Managed WordPress starts at $12.50/mo billed yearly, or $25/mo on monthly billing, and its entry tier is rated for 250,000 visits. Upgrading does not fix the renewal step, which every shared tier carries and which grows with the tier: Turbo renews at $21.95 against its $3.49 sale rate, Starter at $11.95. No shared tier provides dedicated resources either; that starts at $32.95/mo on VPS.

How to actually use ChemiCloud

Accounts activate immediately into cPanel, so the first real decision is whether the site is being built or moved. New sites run through Softaculous one-click installs; existing ones go through the free migration service, which is not self-serve — you open a ticket with the Migrations department from the Client Area and ChemiCloud staff perform the transfer. That service covers up to 200 cPanel accounts, or 10 non-cPanel sites and 10 non-cPanel mailboxes moved via Imapsync, and must be requested within 60 days of order activation.

Beyond that window or those counts it is billed, and it never includes the domain transfer itself. The actual waiting point is DNS: nothing serves until the domain's nameservers are repointed, which the documentation puts at up to 24 hours to propagate worldwide. Managed WordPress adds a step, since instances expect Cloudflare nameservers and display a DNS propagation in progress notice until that is done. The free first-year domain applies only to 36-month shared plans.

Frequently asked questions

What does ChemiCloud actually cost after the discount ends?

$11.95, $17.95 or $21.95 a month for Starter, Pro and Turbo. We have read those same three renewal figures in July 2026, in August 2026, on 17 August 2026 and again on 2 September 2026, while the intro price moved three times. Against the September intro of $1.95 that is roughly a sixfold step, so price the second year, not the first.

Is the September sale a real discount?

It is real and it is dated, but it discounts the introductory term only. Our monthly captures had Starter at $2.49 in both July and August; the tenth-birthday sale cut that to $1.49 and ran into the small hours of 2 September, and the Back to Build campaign that replaced it runs to 30 September at $1.95 on the 36-month term. Nothing about the renewal price changed across any of those readings, and the renewal is where the money is.

What does the reseller plan cost?

From $17.95 a month for Kickstart, read on ChemiCloud's reseller page on 2 September 2026 against a $34.95 regular rate; Grow is $19.95 (regular $45.95) and Expand $34.95 (regular $61.95). All three include cPanel and WHM with a free Blesta licence, and Grow upward adds the WHMCS option. Managed cloud VPS starts at $32.95 a month with 2 CPUs, 4 GB RAM and 80 GB NVMe on the Cloud 1 plan.

What should I weigh before buying IT & Productivity software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
