Zapier’s pricing page, as we read it

What Zapier does

No-code automation platform that connects apps so a trigger in one starts actions in others, with a free plan and paid tiers by task volume.

Professional adds multi-step Zaps; Team adds shared seats

Professional unlocks multi-step Zaps and premium apps, and Team raises the seat count to 25 for shared work. Free is enough only for simple two-step handoffs under 100 tasks a month. Upgrading buys capacity and collaboration. Three things stay the same on every paid tier. Pay-per-task billing is on by default for accounts opened after January 2024: a month that runs past the task tier keeps going at a higher per-task rate until it reaches three times the plan's tasks, and then the Zaps pause.

A bigger tier raises that ceiling along with the base allowance; it does not remove it. The help center guarantees only 60 days of Zap history, shown up to 10,000 runs, and names no plan that keeps more, so an audit trail means exporting it yourself. A downgrade to Free lands at the end of the billing cycle and holds every run that uses a premium app until you upgrade again.

Team is the first plan where admins see task usage for every Zap in the account; Professional is one person's account.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Zapier?

    Exit check 2026-11-18; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does Zapier cost?

    Per the pricing page read on 16 November 2026, Free is $0 with 100 tasks a month, Professional starts at $19.99 a month and Team at $69 a month. The page shows a yearly toggle that saves 33%.

    Is there a free plan?

    Yes. Free covers unlimited Zaps, Tables and Forms, but only two-step Zaps and 100 tasks a month.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes. A new account is enrolled in a 14-day trial of Professional, and no card is needed.

    What counts as a task?

    Any successful action. Triggers, filters, paths and errored steps do not count, per the help center.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. You keep your paid plan until the end of the billing cycle.

    What happens if my Zaps use more tasks than my plan includes?

    They keep running, at a higher price, up to a hard stop. Per the pricing FAQ and the help article on pay-per-task billing (both read 16 November 2026), a paid account that passes its task limit switches to pay-per-task billing for the rest of the period. That is on by default for accounts opened after January 2024. The per-task rate is higher than the plan's own tasks, and Zapier sends you to your Billing settings for the exact figure instead of publishing it. Usage stops at three times the plan's task limit (a 750-task Professional plan tops out at 2,250 tasks), and then Zaps pause until the next period. On Professional and Team only the account owner can switch it off, and usage run up before that goes on the next invoice.

    What changes if I downgrade or move from yearly to monthly billing?

    A downgrade takes effect at the end of the billing cycle and is scheduled only after you finish checkout; the help article on plan changes (read 16 November 2026) tells you to check for the banner with the change date. On Free, Zaps that use premium apps stop and their runs are held, and you can replay them only after upgrading again. Moving from annual to monthly drops the 33% annual discount. Upgrades apply at once, are credited per day, reset the billing date and the task count, and charge any pay-per-task usage at that moment. We found no published refund policy: the refund link on the pricing page led to the plan-change article on 16 November 2026, so whether Zapier refunds unused time is not confirmed.

    How long does Zapier keep a record of what my Zaps did?

    Sixty days. The help article on Zap history (read 16 November 2026) guarantees at most 60 days of run data and displays up to 10,000 runs, and tells you to export the history regularly if you need longer records. On Team and Enterprise, admins and owners see task usage for every Zap in the account; members see only the Zaps they own or that are shared with them.

    What do we not know?

    Which billing period the starting prices assume, what extra tasks cost, and how reliable Zaps are at scale. Our test did not cover reliability at scale.
