Melio: what it costs and who should skip it

Bill payments for small businesses by bank transfer or card, syncing with your accounting tools. The free Go plan covers five ACH transfers a month, then $0.50 each; Core is about $25/month. Card payments carry a flat 2.9%, which only pays off when you are deliberately floating cash.

What to check before you buy

The free plan is genuinely useful for low bill volume, but the value case narrows as you scale into paid tiers — and the 2.9% card fee only makes sense when you're deliberately floating cash on a card for rewards or timing.

The pricing, as we read it

(re-verified 2026-08-17 on their own pricing page) A free Go plan, $0 forever, with 5 free ACH payments a month and $0.50 for each one after that. Core is $25/mo, or $20 billed annually, with 20 free ACH a month; Boost $55/mo, or $44 annually, with 50 free ACH; Unlimited $80/mo.

The detail our earlier note missed: every paid plan adds $10 a month per additional user, or $8 on annual billing, so a three-person finance team on Core is $45 a month rather than $25. Card payments carry a flat 2.9% fee and check and wire payments have their own charges.

There is a 30-day trial.

When the paid tier is worth it

The payoff from upgrading tracks bill volume more than anything else. On the free Go plan you get 5 free ACH payments a month, then $0.50 for each additional one. At that price, moving up only pays for itself once your monthly ACH count is high enough that free-tier overage fees would exceed the subscription cost itself — or once you need features Go simply doesn't offer.

Core ($25/mo, $20/mo billed annually) includes 20 free ACH a month, Boost ($55/mo, $44/mo annually) includes 50, and Unlimited ($80/mo, $64/mo annually) includes unlimited free ACH, so heavy bill-pay volume is where the paid tiers earn their keep. The sharper breakpoint for growing teams is per-seat math: Core and Boost each add $10/mo per additional user ($8/mo billed annually), so a business adding several teammates can find Unlimited's flat unlimited-user pricing cheaper than stacking seats on a lower tier.

What upgrading does not touch: the 2.9% card payment fee is flat across every tier, and per-item check and wire fees apply no matter which plan you're on.

What the price has done

Melio's published ladder held steady over the summer: our July and August 2026 captures are identical — $0, $25 (or $20 annually), $55 (or $44) and the same $10 and $8 per-additional-user lines — and the page returned the same figures on 17 August 2026.

How we would start

Getting started means creating an account with your business details (legal name, industry, address) and personal details, then connecting a way to pay — a bank account verified instantly through Plaid or via micro-deposits, or a card. Vendors get added the same way, with either their bank details for direct deposit or just a mailing address for a check.

The first real payment happens from the Pay dashboard: start a new payment, enter the vendor, bill amount, invoice details and due date, then choose how and when the vendor gets paid. Where people get stuck in practice: skipping the micro-deposit bank verification step blocks payments until it's completed.

QuickBooks users also report the accounting sync breaking down — bills not appearing inside Melio, the bill-pay screen stuck on "loading," or payments not posting back to the books afterward. Support is only staffed during U.S.

Eastern business hours, so anything that goes wrong outside that window tends to sit unresolved until the next day.

Who it is for

Best for small businesses that want to pay bills by bank transfer or card and get paid, starting free with no subscription.

Skip it if you already run AP through robust accounting software with bill-pay built in — a second tool adds little.

What to weigh it against

The comparison that matters is Bill.com — the per-seat AP platform for bigger finance teams.

Questions buyers actually ask

What does Melio cost?

The Go plan is free forever with 5 ACH payments a month, then $0.50 each. Core is $25 a month ($20 annually) with 20 free ACH, Boost $55 ($44) with 50 free ACH, and Unlimited $80. Read on 17 August 2026.

What does it cost for a team?

More than the plan price, and this is the part most summaries leave out: each additional user is $10 a month, or $8 on annual billing, on top of the plan. Core with three users is $45 a month billed monthly, not $25. Count the seats before comparing with a flat-fee competitor.

Are the payments themselves free?

ACH payments are free up to your plan's allowance and $0.50 each after it. Card payments cost a flat 2.9%, and cheques and wires carry their own fees. For a business paying mostly by ACH the effective cost is close to the subscription; for one paying suppliers by card, the 2.9% will dwarf it.
