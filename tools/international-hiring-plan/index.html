Three ways to pay someone abroad

Employer of Record (EOR): the provider employs the person in a country where you have no company, and runs payroll, tax and the employment contract there. Global payroll: you already have a company in that country, and the provider only runs payroll for it. Contractors: the person invoices you; contractor management handles contracts and payments, and Contractor of Record also takes on the risk that the person is legally an employee.
