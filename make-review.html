What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Free is capped at 1,000 credits and a 15-minute interval. Credits expire at the end of the term. We have not run a workflow on a paid plan, so reliability and real credit use are unknown to us.

What Make does

Visual no-code automation platform that connects apps and AI models into multi-step scenarios, with a free plan and paid tiers priced by monthly credits.

Core adds per-minute scheduling and unlimited scenarios

Core at $15 a month (10,000 credits) adds unlimited active scenarios, scheduling down to the minute and the Make API. Pro at $26 adds priority execution, custom variables and log search. Teams at $47 adds team roles and shared templates. Most people start on Free to see how many credits a real workflow burns, then move to Core once they need minute-level scheduling or more than the free credits. What a bigger plan does not fix: credits are use-it-or-lose-it.

Make has a no cash refund policy, unused credits do not follow you to Free when you cancel, and on Core even a yearly plan hands them out month by month, so buying a bigger annual tier as a reserve only works on Pro and Teams. Extra credits bought on top cost 25% more than plan credits (help centre, read 18 November 2026), which makes picking the right tier cheaper than topping up.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Make?

    Exit check 2026-11-18; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does Make cost?

    Per the pricing page read on 18 November 2026, in US dollars a month with the credit slider at 10,000 credits: Free is $0 with 1,000 credits, Core is $15, Pro is $26 and Teams is $47. Enterprise is custom. Billed yearly the same slider shows Core at $11, Pro at $20 and Teams at $36 a month.

    What is a credit?

    Credits are the unit Make bills in. The page says most actions cost 1 credit, router and error-handler modules cost nothing, and code runs in the Make Code App cost 2 credits per second. Credits expire at the end of the billing term.

    Is there a free plan?

    Yes. Free costs $0 and includes 1,000 credits a month, the visual workflow builder, 3,500+ apps, routers and filters, and a 15-minute minimum interval between runs. The home page says no credit card is required and the free plan has no time limit.

    What does Core add over Free?

    Unlimited active scenarios, scheduling down to the minute, higher data transfer limits and access to the Make API.

    What do Pro and Teams add?

    Pro adds priority scenario execution, custom variables and full-text search of the execution log. Teams adds team roles and shared scenario templates.

    Why do older reviews show $9 and $16?

    Make changed its pricing model to credits and the price depends on the slider. Our first read of the page showed $9 for Core and $16 for Pro at another slider position, so always check the credit count next to a price.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. The page says you can cancel at any time, the paid plan runs until the end of the term and the account then drops to Free.

    What happens to unused credits if I cancel or change plan?

    Per Make's help centre, read 18 November 2026: after you cancel, the paid plan runs to the end of the billing cycle and the organization then drops to Free with the standard 1,000 credits a month; unused credits are not carried over, on monthly or annual billing. Free supports one team, so you pick which team to keep. Make has a no cash refund policy: on an upgrade your unused credits become a discount on the new plan, on a downgrade they move to the new plan. Only an Owner or Admin can cancel.

    Do I get a year of credits up front on annual billing?

    On Pro and Teams, yes: the help centre (read 18 November 2026) says an annual subscription allocates the whole year's credits at once to use flexibly. Core is the exception: it always hands out credits monthly, even when billed yearly. Extra credits cost 25% more than the credits in your plan, are sold in steps of 1,000 by card only, and expire at the end of the billing cycle; on Core with annual billing they expire at each monthly reset.

    Can several people share one Make account, and can I take my scenarios with me?

    Below the Teams plan an organization has one team and you cannot manage roles within it, per the Teams article read 18 November 2026, so separate access for different people starts at Teams. Scenarios can be exported as blueprint JSON files that hold the modules, settings and mapped values; connections are not included and have to be recreated after import.
