Deel: what it costs and who should skip it

Global hiring, payroll and EOR platform — pay contractors and employees compliantly in 150+ countries, from $49 per contractor per month, with HR tools and US PEO options.

What to check before you buy

Every price is a ‘starting at’ and every button says ‘book a demo’ — the real bill depends on countries, benefits and add-ons, and only firms up in a sales conversation. And know what EOR money buys: at $599+/month per employee you’re paying for compliance risk transfer, not software — worth it in unfamiliar jurisdictions, expensive where you could open your own entity.

The pricing, as we read it

(re-verified 2026-08-17) Pricing is per worker and demo-led, and each product is priced separately. HR and hiring tools start at $14 per worker a month. Contractor management is $49 per contractor a month, or $325 per month per Contractor of Record if you want Deel to carry the compliance risk.

US PEO starts at $125 per employee a month. And EOR, where Deel employs your international hire, starts at $599 per employee a month. There is a free demo and final quotes come through sales.

The spread is the point: the same headcount costs $14 or $599 a month depending on which relationship you need, so identify the employment model before comparing vendors.

When the paid tier is worth it

There is no plan ladder here, only a product ladder, and the steps are enormous: HR and hiring from $14 per worker a month, contractor management from $49 per contractor, Contractor of Record at $325, US PEO from $125 per employee and EOR from $599. The same person costs $49 or $325 depending on who carries the misclassification risk, and that step buys no extra software.

That is the honest frame for every upgrade here: above the $49 tier you buy liability transfer rather than features. Deel makes the choice explicit at contract creation, where you pick either coverage up to $25,000 or full misclassification coverage.

What paying more does not do is change the underlying relationship. If the work is really employment - set hours, your equipment, your direction - no coverage tier turns it into contracting; it only decides who argues about it.

Nor does EOR give you a local entity: at $599 per employee a month across 130+ countries you are renting one, which pays while you test a market and gets expensive once that country holds a real team. Every figure is a starting-at behind a demo, so none of them is a quote.

How we would start

Adding a first contractor is a nine-step wizard: the People icon, then Add People, then Contractor, starting either from a blank contract or a pre-filled template. You choose the group and entity, enter the worker's name, email and country of residence, select the agreement type and set a Scope of Work, which Deel AI will draft if you have no existing text to reuse.

Compensation is where the shape gets set. Fixed Rate pays weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly or monthly; Pay As You Go bills hourly, daily, weekly or per task; Milestone releases against deliverables. One detail helps a fast start: the docs say a Pay As You Go rate is optional at creation, but the contractor cannot submit work until a rate exists, and adding it later is an amendment that needs a signature.

Then pick coverage - up to $25,000 or full misclassification coverage - and sign. The contract only activates once both parties have accepted, so allow for the contractor's turnaround; you can switch the signing order, and up to five signers can be required in sequence.

Who it is for

Best for companies hiring internationally without local entities — contractors paid compliantly in 150+ countries, or full EOR employment where you need someone on the ground fast.

Skip it if your team is all-domestic (a local payroll tool is far cheaper), or you hire enough people in one country that opening your own entity beats paying per-employee EOR fees forever.

What to weigh it against

The comparison that matters is Gusto — Gusto is the friendly US-payroll incumbent for domestic teams; Deel is built for global — contractors, EOR and payroll across 150+ countries. Pick by where your people are.

Questions buyers actually ask

What does Deel cost?

It depends entirely on the product: $14 per worker a month for HR and hiring tools, $49 per contractor for contractor management, $325 per Contractor of Record, $125 per US PEO employee and $599 per EOR employee. Read on 17 August 2026.

What is the difference between the $49 and $325 contractor options?

Who carries the misclassification risk. At $49 a month you manage the contractor yourself through Deel's platform; at $325 Deel becomes the Contractor of Record and takes on the compliance exposure. The $276 difference is an insurance premium, so price it against what a misclassification finding would cost you in that country.

Why is EOR so much more expensive?

Because it is a different service: at $599 per employee a month Deel is the legal employer in a country where you have no entity, handling payroll, taxes, benefits and termination law. Compared with the cost of incorporating and running a local entity it is often cheaper; compared with a contractor arrangement it is many times the price, which is why the classification question comes first.
