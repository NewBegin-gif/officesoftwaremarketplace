Beautiful.ai Review (2026)

By Daan Haket · Tested hands-on · written with an AI assistant · who does what

The smart-template engine is the feature and the fence: decks look polished with zero design skill precisely because the system constrains you, so pixel-perfect custom layouts and heavy brand exceptions fight the tool. Throughout this review we weigh it against PowerPoint.

Best for

founders, sales and small teams that want consistently good-looking decks fast, where design consistency matters more than pixel-level control.

Skip it if

you need intricate custom layouts or complex animations (designers want full control), or your whole company workflow is deeply PowerPoint-native.

We tested it · 2026-09-28

On 28 September 2026 we gave Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Prezi and Canva the same brief for a ten-slide investor deck: eleven facts about a made-up bakery chain and the rule to add no other numbers. We checked every deck against 15 tests written down before the first one was generated. Beautiful.ai offered us only its 14-day Pro trial, which asks for a payment card and bills a year of Pro on day 14; we cancelled it the same morning.

  • It showed an outline within seconds and a finished deck about two and a half minutes after the prompt, with no numbers of its own.
  • The outline had a [MARKET SIZE] placeholder, but the finished market slide dropped it (without inventing a figure) and added Amsterdam as a target city.
  • Both charts carry the right data but read oddly: the revenue line's axis labels are rounded to 0 and 1, and use of funds is shown in euros (280,000, 80,000, 40,000) instead of the 70/20/10 we gave.

When the paid plan is worth it

Beautiful.ai's pricing page lists no free tier. Pro is $14.50 /mo, billed annually, for a single seat (it was $12 until August 2026); Team is $40 /MO/User billed annually or $50 billed monthly for 2-20 seats; Enterprise is only "Contact sales for pricing". On 29 September 2026 the Team card itself read "$40 /MO (includes 3 users)" with "fixed pricing for up to three users", which contradicts the per-user wording in the same page's FAQ, so get the seat price in writing before you budget a team.

Paying monthly instead of annually means $45 for the same Pro plan, sold as a one-off for ad hoc projects. The widest jump in the table is Pro to Team, and what that money buys is workspace machinery rather than better output: shared libraries, roles and admin control. The design engine and editable PowerPoint export are already on Pro, so a solo user gets little from Team. Upgrading does not fix the commercial fine print.

The help center states that team seats are non-refundable and cannot be transferred between teams, so each team buys its own; annual subscribers may only request a partial refund within 24 hours of purchase or renewal, and monthly subscriptions are not refundable. Downgrading lands you on Basic, capped at 100 slides, where folders are removed, secured sharing reverts to public, and Beautiful.ai branding returns to playback, sharing and export.

How to actually use Beautiful.ai

The vendor route to a first deck is a four-stage flow: click Create Presentation, pick Create With AI, then enter or paste a prompt, optionally attaching documents, PDFs or links. From there you edit the generated text-only outline, set visual preferences such as theme, image source and language, and click Generate Presentation. Slide AI handles per-slide regeneration afterwards.

Common Beautiful.ai setup mistakes

Where Beautiful.ai setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above.

The friction sits around that flow, not in it.

  • The 14-day trial requires a debit or credit card up front and rolls into a paid subscription when it expires, and cancelling requires a desktop or laptop.
  • PowerPoint import is a per-slide picking flow inside an existing presentation, with the docs warning not to close or refresh the tab while it runs.
  • Export failures are pinned on broken slides and browser cache.
  • SSO is Owner-only, needs domain verification and SAML endpoint plus issuer details, and Beautiful.ai warns that existing users get locked out if Strict SSO is enabled before they are provisioned in the IdP.
  • The API is early access, returning 403 when access is not enabled.

Frequently asked questions

Can Beautiful.ai import an existing PowerPoint deck?

Yes, but it is not a bulk conversion. Per the vendor documentation, import is a per-slide picking flow inside an existing presentation, and the docs warn you not to close or refresh the tab while it runs; export failures are pinned on broken slides and browser cache. Editable PowerPoint export is already included on Pro ($14.50 a month billed annually since August 2026; it was $12 when verified on 11 August 2026). If round-tripping decks with colleagues is your actual workflow, test that loop inside the 14-day trial before you pay for a year.

Is the $45 one-off cheaper than subscribing?

For up to three months, yes. Beautiful.ai sells the Pro plan month to month at $45 for ad hoc projects, against $14.50 a month on the annual plan, which is $174 for the year. Three months month-to-month is $135; from the fourth month the annual plan is the cheaper of the two. Both figures were on the vendor pricing page on 2 September 2026 and again on 29 September 2026.

What happens to my decks if I stop paying?

You land on Basic, and the help centre is specific about what that costs you: a cap of 100 slides, folders removed, secured sharing reverting to public, and Beautiful.ai branding returning to playback, sharing and export. Worth knowing before you move a client-facing deck library in — and worth reading alongside the refund terms, which make annual refunds a partial-refund window of 24 hours after purchase or renewal, with monthly subscriptions not refundable.
