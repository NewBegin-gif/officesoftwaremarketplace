Frequently asked questions

What IT tools does a small business really need?

At minimum: reliable hosting, security (a password manager, ideally a VPN), and backup. Add personal-productivity tools once the foundations are solid — losing data or getting breached costs far more than any subscription.

Is it worth paying for security and backup software?

Almost always. The cost of a single breach or lost drive dwarfs a yearly subscription. Look for zero-knowledge encryption for sensitive files and automated, off-site backup so it happens without you remembering.
