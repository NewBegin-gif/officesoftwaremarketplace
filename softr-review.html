The dearer plan charges double per extra user

The free plan is unusually usable: unlimited apps, up to 3 builders, 5 app users, 5,000 database records, 500 workflow actions and 5 AI credits a month. Paid tiers on yearly billing are Basic $19 a month, Pro $99 and Business $329, with Enterprise on custom limits. Extra builder seats are priced per tier at $5, $10 and $20 a month (re-verified 2026-09-07). Note what the page does with billing periods: it opens on the Yearly tab - the markup carries that selection, not just the styling - and advertises two months off, so every figure you see is already the discounted one and paying monthly costs more: the plan panel inside the app showed $25, $119 and $395 a month on 21 October 2026, about a third more than $19 on Basic and about a fifth more on Pro and Business, our arithmetic.

The number that decides your bill is the app-user allowance, and it is published on the comparison table rather than being something you have to ask for. Softr counts two populations. Builders make the app; app users log into it. App users then split again: Team users share a business email domain with one of your builders, and everyone else - including every Gmail address - is a Client user.

The allowances run Team 5 plus Client 5 on Basic, Team 10 plus Client 50 on Pro, and Team 30 plus Client 100 on Business. Builders can be added as app users at no charge. Beyond the allowance, extra app users start at $1 a month on Pro and $2 a month on Business. That is worth reading twice: the plan costing three and a half times more charges double per additional user.

If you expect to run well past your allowance, the cheaper plan is also the cheaper meter, and the reason to buy Business becomes its other limits - 1M records against Pro's 500K, 25K workflow actions against 10K - rather than its user economics. The ladder itself is steep: $19 to $99 is more than five times, $99 to $329 more than three. Builder seats quadruple across it too, though as a share of the plan they get cheaper, from 26% of Basic to 6% of Business.

That distinction settles what this tool is for. With a known, counted audience - an internal tool, a client portal, a members' area - the meter is predictable. With an open signup it is your growth curve. Start on the free plan; at 5,000 records and three builders it is enough to build something real. And if you are a non-profit or in education, claim the stated 50% discount, which takes Business from $329 to $164.50.

How to actually use Softr

Count your two user populations before you build, because Softr meters them separately and the split is not obvious. A builder is someone who makes the app, an app user is someone who logs into it. App users then divide into Team users, who share a business email domain with one of your builders, and Client users, which is everyone else - every Gmail address included. Check the overage rate against the plan before you climb.

Additional app users start at $1 a month on Pro but $2 on Business, so if you expect to run past your allowance, moving up costs you twice as much per user. Buy Business for its record and workflow-action limits, not for its user economics. Then build the real thing. A new account starts on a 7-day trial of Business and drops back to Free on its own ('Automatic when trial ends', the app's plan panel said on 21 October 2026), so check after a week which limits you are on.

Unlimited apps, three builders and 5,000 records is enough for a working internal tool or client portal, and the five-app-user cap is the only thing that stops you. Two last things. The page opens on the Yearly tab, so $19, $99 and $329 already assume annual billing; the plan panel inside the app showed $25, $119 and $395 a month on 21 October 2026. And non-profits and education should claim the stated 50% discount rather than assuming it is applied automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What does Softr cost?

Free for a small app (3 builders, 5 app users, 5,000 records), then $19, $99 and $329 a month on yearly billing for Basic, Pro and Business. Monthly billing costs more - the page advertises two months off for paying yearly. Read on 7 September 2026. Inside the app on 21 October 2026 the plan panel showed $25, $119 and $395 a month, and a new account started on a 7-day Business trial that falls back to Free on its own.

What counts as a billable user?

Two different things, which is the most common budgeting mistake here. Builders log into Softr Studio to build, and cost $5, $10 or $20 a month each beyond the one, two or three included. App users are the end users who log into what you built, and they split again: Team users share a business email domain with one of your builders, everyone else (Gmail included) is a Client user. The allowances are Team 5 + Client 5 on Basic, Team 10 + Client 50 on Pro and Team 30 + Client 100 on Business. Builders can be added as app users free.

Which plan is cheapest if my app grows?

Not necessarily the bigger one. Extra app users beyond the allowance start at $1 a month on Pro but $2 a month on Business, so the dearer plan meters growth at double the rate. Buy Business for its record limit (1M against Pro's 500K) and workflow actions (25K against 10K) rather than for its user economics. Non-profits and education can claim a stated 50% discount, which takes Business from $329 to $164.50.

Can I connect my own data source through a REST API?

Softr lists it: its homepage (read 3 November 2026) names REST API among the data sources, to 'connect your apps to any external tool or data source'. Which plan you need is less clear. On softr.io/pricing the same day, 'Custom code & API connectors' first appears on Pro ($99 a month on yearly billing), and Pro lists +40 integrations against +38 on Free and Basic; the page does not say whether the REST API source is one of the Pro-only items. Do not confuse it with the Softr API & MCP line, which is about reaching your Softr data from outside and which, per the page, may have endpoints limited to higher plans. Not confirmed: whether REST API works on Basic.
