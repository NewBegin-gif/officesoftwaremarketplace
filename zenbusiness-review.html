Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 27 Nov 2026

Formation itself is $0 plus the state's fee; that applies to LLC, S corp and C corp filings alike, and the state fee is separate, varies by state and is not shown until you pick one. Everything else on this page is priced as its own line; the page also points to Starter, Pro and Premium formation packages 'starting at $0 + state fees', which this check did not price.

The recurring items are registered agent at $199/year, Worry-Free Compliance Filing at $199/year, and Advanced Compliance Protection at $299/year. One-time filings: operating agreement $99, EIN $99, business document templates $99, rush filing $79 and DBA registration $95 plus state fees. Then business licence report $149, amendment $100, certificate of good standing $100, annual report filing $100 per report, and New York publication $200 for the state that requires it.

Full pricing notes (2 more paragraphs)

Three lines now carry terms on the page: the registered agent is billed after your LLC is approved, with no per-document fees and cancellation by phone; the EIN is $149 together with business documents and does not renew; and Worry-Free Compliance covers the annual report plus up to two amendments a year, is free for year 1 with Starter and included in Pro and Premium, with state fees extra.

There is a second catalogue alongside the legal one: the business website builder is now a 30-day free trial that renews at $15 a month unless cancelled ($129 a year on 22 September 2026), eCommerce website $279/year, business email address $25/year, domain $25/year, domain privacy $10/year, logo kit $60 and Money Pro Bookkeeping $30/month. The $5-a-month business banking line is no longer on the page.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

A registered agent is a legal requirement in every state, and theirs is $199/year, so the realistic first-year cost of 'the $0 LLC' is the state fee plus $199, before compliance filing at another $199/year. Price the second year, not the first. The second thing to hold in view is that the state fee is the largest and most variable number in the whole exercise, and ZenBusiness cannot change it.

It is set by your state, ranges widely, and their own page sends you to a lookup rather than quoting it. Anyone comparing formation services on the advertised price alone is comparing the small half of the bill.

When the paid plan is worth it

The upgrade question here is not a plan tier, it is which recurring line you keep. Worry-Free Compliance at $199/year covers the annual report plus up to two amendments a year, with state fees extra (products page, read 27 November 2026). Filed one at a time, the annual report is $100 per report and an amendment is $100. So the maths only works if you expect at least one amendment on top of the annual report, or you value not tracking the deadline.

It is free for year 1 with the Starter formation package and included in Pro and Premium, so check your package before paying for it separately. Advanced Compliance Protection at $299/year adds unlimited amendment filings and a money-back guarantee on penalties for missed compliance events: that guarantee is the real difference between the two, so read what it covers before paying the extra $100. On filings alone, the extra $100 only pays back from a third amendment in a year.

Neither subscription pays the state's own fees. Registered agent at $199/year is the one line most businesses cannot skip, unless you are willing to use your own address publicly and be available during business hours to receive legal documents.

How to actually use ZenBusiness

Look up your state's filing fee and processing time first: their own page has the lookup, and that number will usually be larger than anything ZenBusiness charges you. Decide the registered agent question before you file, because it is a required field on the filing itself: your own address is free but becomes public record and ties you to business hours, theirs is $199/year. Then file at $0 and add nothing else on day one.

The EIN at $99 is the add-on worth pausing on: it is required to open a business bank account or hire, and the IRS issues it directly at no charge, so you are paying to not deal with the IRS yourself. Standard filing is included; rush at $79 only matters if you have a launch date. If you are forming in New York, budget the $200 publication line separately: that state requires a newspaper announcement and it catches people out.

Frequently asked questions

Which of the two compliance subscriptions should I take, if either?

Price them against the a-la-carte filings rather than against each other. Read 27 November 2026, Worry-Free Compliance is $199 a year and covers the annual report plus up to two amendments a year; Advanced Compliance Protection is $299 and adds 24/7 AI monitoring, advanced support, unlimited amendment filings and a money-back guarantee on any penalties for missed compliance events. Filed individually an annual report is $100 per report and an amendment is $100, so the $199 tier breaks even once you add one amendment to the annual report. On filings alone the extra $100 for the top tier only pays back from a third amendment in a year; below that, what it buys is the monitoring and the penalty guarantee. If you expect one annual report and nothing else, neither subscription beats paying the $100 when it falls due: what you would be buying is the deadline being someone else's problem. Worry-Free is free for year 1 with the Starter formation package and included in Pro and Premium. Read what the penalty guarantee actually covers before you treat it as the tiebreaker.

The website and email products are cheap. Is that a reason to buy them here?

Treat them as a separate purchase, because they are a separate catalogue that happens to share an invoice. Re-verified 27 November 2026: the business website builder is a 30-day free trial that then renews at $15 a month unless cancelled ($129 a year when we read it on 22 September), eCommerce website $279 a year, business email address $25 a year, domain $25 a year, domain privacy $10 a year, logo kit $60 and Money Pro Bookkeeping $30 a month; the $5-a-month business banking line from September is no longer listed. The website trial is the one to put in your diary, because it renews unless you cancel. None of these is discounted for being bought alongside the formation, and each competes with general-purpose providers on its own merits, so compare them one at a time rather than as a bundle. There is one structural point worth a thought: buying the domain here puts your registered agent, your public filing address and your DNS with the same vendor, which is convenient until you want to leave. The legal lines and the web lines are independent; you can take the $199 registered agent and none of this.

What is unavoidable in the first year?

Two things, and only one of them is ZenBusiness's. The state's filing fee is the larger and more variable number in the whole exercise, it is set by your state rather than by ZenBusiness, and their page sends you to a lookup instead of quoting it, so it is not visible until you pick a state. The other is the registered agent, which is a legal requirement in every state; theirs is $199 a year, and the alternative is using your own address, which becomes public record and ties you to being available during business hours. Everything else on the price list is optional, including the $99 EIN, the $99 operating agreement, the $79 rush filing and both compliance subscriptions. New York is the one jurisdiction with a third unavoidable line: $200 for the newspaper publication that state requires. Re-verified 27 November 2026; the page now adds that the registered agent fee is billed after your LLC is approved.

Can I use the Velo planning assistant without buying a formation package?

The pricing and products page says so. Read on 9 November 2026, it lists Velo, ZenBusiness's AI guide for business planning, under a "Dream and Plan" heading with the line "ZenBusiness Velo is free to use", next to a free business name generator and apart from the paid formation and compliance services. Using it does not file anything for you: formation, the compliance subscriptions and the per-report filings are separate purchases. The page does not say whether Velo needs an account or what it does with the business details you give it.
