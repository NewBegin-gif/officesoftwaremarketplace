What Pdfdodo does

Converts PDF bank statements, invoices and receipts, including scans and handwriting, into CSV or Excel tables with OCR, with an API for automated workflows; plans are metered by pages per month.

Every plan has every feature; you buy pages

The FAQ says the free plan gets all the features of the paid plans and only the number of pages differs, so the tiers are a page meter. Each analysed page uses one credit: a 10-page bank statement costs 10 of the month's pages. Billed monthly that works out at about 8.3 cents a page on Solo, 5.9 cents on Startup and 3.3 cents on Business at full use, our arithmetic from the plan prices and page counts.

Two things change besides the page count. Free caps each PDF at 5 MB and the paid plans at 15 MB, with no upload limit on Business; and Business gets priority support where Solo and Startup list same-day support. Yearly billing is shown as two months free, which matches the Solo ($250), Startup ($590) and Business ($990) cards as read on 6 November 2026. The Solo card read $20 beside $200 on 16 October, so it has been repriced: check the amount at checkout before you pay for a year.

    How to actually use Pdfdodo

    Start on the free plan, which the FAQ says has every feature with 25 pages a month, and run the documents you actually have to convert: sign in, upload a PDF, click Analyze, and export the tables to CSV or Excel. Use your hardest file first, such as a scanned or photographed bank statement, because the FAQ says the OCR handles scans and handwriting and asks for images of at least 150 DPI.

    Count pages, not files, when you pick a plan: one page is one credit, and you get an error once the month's limit is used up. If your statements are not in English, check the FAQ list first; it names German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese besides English. Cancelling is done by email, which the FAQ says is processed within 24 hours.

    Can I get a refund?

    It is unclear. The FAQ says Pdfdodo gives no refunds, partial or full; section 8 of the terms says refunds are issued within 30 days of the original purchase. Ask support in writing before you buy a yearly plan.

    Does it work on scanned or handwritten PDFs?

    Pdfdodo says yes: the FAQ says it uses OCR on scanned documents, printed or handwritten, and accepts PDF, PNG, JPEG and TIFF up to 15 MB. For images it asks for at least 150 DPI.

    What happens to my uploaded documents?

    The FAQ says uploads are encrypted, stored only for the analysis with a storage provider holding PCI, ISO and SOC certifications, and deleted once the analysis is complete.

    Can I take the extracted tables out of Pdfdodo?

    Yes, as files. Pdfdodo's homepage, read on 25 October 2026, says it extracts data from PDFs and lets you download it in CSV or Excel format. The homepage does not describe an API or a direct link into accounting software, so treat those as not confirmed and plan on a download-and-import step.
