We tested it · 2026-11-02

Tested by our team on 2 November 2026 on the free trial: Daan created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in Daan's browser. We imported two made-up contacts from a CSV file, then exported them again to CSV. We did not open the checkout.

How we tested

  1. Imported two made-up contacts from a CSV with accented names and a note
  2. Mapped each column by hand
  3. Exported both contacts to CSV and compared the result with the input
  • The dashboard showed '30 days left', matching the 30-day trial in the help centre, and the workspace already held sample contacts and candidates.
  • The importer started every column on 'Don't Import This Column' and had the header row switched off by default, so each field has to be mapped by hand. We found no custom fields in the mapping list.
  • The import warned that 'all the special characters in FirstName and LastName will be removed', yet both exported first names kept their accents.

Pro is €79 per user a month billed annually or €95 monthly, with 100 credits per user a month. Business is €109 annually or €135 monthly with 200 credits, and adds the resume formatting agent, multichannel sequences across email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, white-labelled pages, the open API, SSO, IP whitelisting and 30-day audit logs. Enterprise is €189 annually or €225 monthly with 2,000 credits, Recruit Craft (the career-site builder) included and a named account manager.

Credits are pooled across all users in the account and pay for AI sourcing (3 credits per 100 profiles), data enrichment (1 to 4 credits per record), job multiposting (25 credits per job), LinkedIn messaging (150 credits per user a month), WhatsApp (100 per user a month), advanced analytics (300 per user a month) and workflow automation; extra credits can be bought.

The help centre says annual billing charges the discounted rate upfront for the whole year. There is a 30-day free trial. Recruit CRM told us it has no discount code for our readers (October 2026). The plan comparison re-read on 1 November 2026 lists the Recruit CRM MCP (for Claude and other AI assistants) as included in Pro, Business and Enterprise, and data migration as a free CSV import tool with assisted migration paid on every plan. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What Recruit CRM does

Applicant tracking system and client CRM for recruitment agencies, with a Chrome sourcing extension, hiring manager portal, AI sourcing and multichannel outreach on a shared credit pool.

Business buys the outreach and the API

Pro at €79 per user a month (annual) covers the ATS and CRM itself: pipelines, the hiring manager portal, the Chrome sourcing extension, two-way email and calendar sync. Business at €109 is the step most growing agencies weigh, because it adds multichannel sequences across email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, the open API (the onboarding guide says API access needs Business or higher), SSO and audit logs, and doubles the record limit per user from 10,000 to 20,000 candidates, companies and contacts each.

The credits are the hidden part of the bill. LinkedIn messaging alone uses 150 credits per user a month and Pro includes 100, so a recruiter who wants LinkedIn sync on Pro is already buying credits. Map which credit features you will use before you pick a tier.

How to actually use Recruit CRM

The 30-day trial is enough for a real pilot, but the help centre caps trial records at 50 candidates, 50 companies, 50 contacts and 4 jobs, so test the workflow, not your whole database. Connect your mailbox and your Google or Outlook calendar first, set up your profile, then customise the hiring pipeline stages to match how your desk works. Import existing candidates and companies from Excel or CSV once you have decided to stay; the onboarding guide allows up to 10,000 records per import.

Invite teammates last, because you pay per provisioned seat, and check which of your daily actions consume credits.

Frequently asked questions

Can you export your data from Recruit CRM?

Exit check 2026-11-02; route excerpt: Contacts > select two > More > Export > Fields to Export > Export to CSV; came along (excerpts): Two exported contacts, IDs 2 and 3 / Unicode in both first names; lost (excerpts): Contact Note text absent; re-import: not tested.

What does Recruit CRM cost?

Read on 25 October 2026 (prices shown in euros): Pro €79 per user a month billed annually or €95 monthly, Business €109 or €135, Enterprise €189 or €225. Each plan includes credits per user per month (100, 200 or 2,000) for features such as AI sourcing, enrichment and LinkedIn messaging. There is a 30-day free trial.

Is there a Recruit CRM discount code?

No. Recruit CRM's partner team told us in October 2026 that there is no discount code at the moment. The only saving on the pricing page is annual billing, which the page labels 'Save up to 19%'.

Can I get a refund if I cancel?

The subscription article says no refunds are issued for any unused portion of a subscription. Changes and payment issues have to be raised before the renewal date to avoid the renewal charge; after cancelling you are not charged from the next billing date.

Are candidates and contacts really unlimited?

The pricing comparison says unlimited jobs, candidates and contacts, but the help centre's subscription article, read the same day, lists record limits: 10,000 candidates, companies and contacts each per user on Pro, 20,000 on Business and 30,000 on Enterprise, pooled across the account. Jobs are unlimited on paid plans.

Do I need Business for the API?

Yes. The onboarding guide says API access requires a Business plan subscription or higher.
