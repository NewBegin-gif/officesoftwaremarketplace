What VideoScribe does

Whiteboard and hand-drawn animation software for explainer and training videos: build scenes from a large image library, add text, music and voiceover, with AI tools for scripts, images and voiceovers; priced per licence by downloads and video length.

Core is the first plan without a watermark

The plan follows from two numbers: how many videos you export a month and how long they run. Lite allows 5 downloads of up to 5 minutes and still watermarks premium images. Core is the first plan with no watermark on videos, 30 downloads of up to 10 minutes and AI voiceover (75 minutes a month), which makes it the realistic plan for business use. Max adds unlimited downloads, 20-minute videos, a brand kit, custom fonts and reseller rights, which the page says are only included in Max.

Annual billing is where the price falls: Lite costs €165 a year against €408 for twelve monthly payments, Core €275 against €660 and Max €350 against €840, our arithmetic from the two views of the page. The catch is that payments are non-refundable, so the annual price is only cheaper if you are sure after the trial.

How to actually use VideoScribe

Use the 7-day trial, which needs no credit card, to build one real video. VideoScribe describes the flow as creating a project, adding images from the library, adding text and audio, applying animations, arranging everything on the timeline, and then previewing and sharing. The trial allows 3 downloads of up to 5 minutes, watermarked, which is enough to judge the style and how long a video takes you. Count the videos you will export each month and their length before you buy; those two limits decide the plan.

On a monthly plan you cancel with 'Cancel subscription' in the Manage menu on the subscription card, at least three business days before the next payment, because payments already made are not refunded.
